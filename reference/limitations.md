# Limitations Note

Include a version of this on every deliverable (Excel cover/notes sheet, Word/PDF footer or opening note). Adapt the wording, but keep the substance — don't compress it into a single throwaway line, since it's the thing that keeps the output from being misread as final/audited financials.

> **Basis of preparation**: These statements are prepared solely from bank statement transactions for the period stated, on a cash-received/cash-paid basis. They do not include debtors, creditors, stock-in-hand, or fixed assets not reflected in this bank account, and so do not represent a complete accrual-basis position. Transaction classification into ledger heads was inferred from bank narration text and is a draft for review, not a certified or audited statement. [If opening balances were assumed: "Opening capital was assumed equal to the bank's opening balance in the absence of other information."] [If entity type was assumed: state which and that it was assumed.]

## What would upgrade this toward real financials

Mention these once, as an offer, not a repeated nag — most users either have this information ready or genuinely don't and don't want to be asked twice:

- Opening balances: capital account, loan balances, bank balance at the start of the period (if not the statement's own opening balance)
- Debtors (amounts owed to the business) and creditors (amounts owed by the business) as of the statement's closing date
- Stock-in-hand valuation as of the closing date
- Fixed assets not purchased through this bank account (or purchased before the period)
- Cash transactions that never touched the bank account
- For a partnership: the profit-sharing ratio, if not already provided
- For a company: confirmation on how owner-style transactions should be treated (remuneration / director's loan / dividend)
